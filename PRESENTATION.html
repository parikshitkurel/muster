01 / Problem Context

Event staffing is a high-stakes multi-constraint problem.

Live production events (concerts, technical summits, festivals) suffer catastrophic operational failure when staffing relies on manual WhatsApp groups and spreadsheets.

1. Rigid Hard Constraints
An event cannot execute without exact role quotas (e.g., exactly 2 Dante Sound Engineers and 1 GrandMA3 Lighting Designer). Single missing roles compromise the entire production.
2. Inviolable Budget Ceilings
Individual rate spikes and travel surcharges quickly lead to budget overruns. Organizers need mathematical guarantees that total crew expenditure remains ≤ budget.
3. Transit & Reliability Risk
Staff travelling from distant locations face transit delays, while unverified freelancers carry a 20%+ no-show probability. There is zero historical reliability accountability.
The Industry Gap: Why Existing Solutions Fail
Generic gig platforms (Upwork/Fiverr) optimize for individual hires rather than cross-role team synergy. Generic AI chatbots hallucinate rates and ignore hard mathematical budget constraints.

04 / Algorithmic Core

Where is the Math? Multi-Objective Optimization

MUSTER calculates candidate alignment through normalized composite multi-objective scoring and hard constraint satisfaction.

Composite Objective Scoring Formula
S_c = (w₁ · M_c) + (w₂ · R_c) + (w₃ · P_c) + (w₄ · C_c)

Where:
• M_c = Skill & Experience Alignment Index [0..100]
• R_c = Verified Historical Reliability Rating [0..100]
• P_c = max(0, 100 - 2 · Distance_km) [Proximity Score]
• C_c = 100 · (1 - (Rate_c - MinRate)/(MaxRate - MinRate))

Weights: w₁=0.40, w₂=0.35, w₃=0.15, w₄=0.10 (∑ w_i = 1.0)
Hard Inviolable Constraints
1. Budget Cap: ∑ (Rate_i × 8 hrs) ≤ Event Budget
2. Spatial Boundary: Distance_i ≤ Event Proximity Radius
3. Rate Ceiling: Rate_i ≤ Max Allowed Role Rate
4. Quota Satisfaction: Selected_k = Required_k for all roles
Zero Hallucination Guarantee
The optimization engine operates deterministically. If no candidate combination satisfies the budget, the system returns a formal constraint violation rather than fabricating unvetted talent.
05 / AI Intelligence Layer

Where is the AI? Gemini 1.5 via Supabase Edge Function

Clear architectural separation: The deterministic engine guarantees mathematical correctness; Google Gemini 1.5 delivers intelligence and explainability.

1. NLP Brief Understanding
Converts natural-language organizer prompts ("Need 2 audio engineers in Mumbai under 1.5L") into structured quotas, rate caps, and budget parameters.
parse_requirements
2. Transparent Explainability
Generates human-readable rationales justifying why each specialist was selected based on skill match, past reliability rating, and transit proximity.
explain_roster
3. Trade-off Synthesis
Explains strategic trade-offs when cycling through alternative rosters (e.g., ₹14,000 cost reduction vs 4% experience delta).
explain_tradeoffs
Privacy & Server-Side Security Guard
The Gemini API key is isolated server-side inside the muster-ai Supabase Edge Function. Request payloads are strictly sanitized: no private phone numbers, emails, or personal passwords are ever exposed to the LLM.
