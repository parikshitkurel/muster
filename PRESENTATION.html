Meet MUSTER.

  • ✓ Receive custom hourly bids from vetted freelancers.
  • ✓ Find valid, constraint-checked crews in seconds.

BALANCED MATCHING CRITERIA

The cheapest person isn't always the best fit.

MUSTER balances multiple factors together.

40%

Skills & Experience

Direct match with role requirements, tools, and certifications.

35%

Reliability Score

Historical show-up rate, punctuality, and organizer ratings.

15%

Transit Distance

Proximity to event venue to prevent traffic and transit delays.

10%

Rate Efficiency

Hourly proposal relative to the event's budgeted rate band.

Result: Reliable, skilled crew members who can actually arrive on time and stay within budget.
HONEST ARCHITECTURE SEPARATION

AI helps. The system checks.

The matching engine checks the rules. Gemini explains the result.

DETERMINISTIC ENGINE

Enforces Hard Rules

• 100% Budget Compliance Guarantee (Cost ≤ Budget)
• Fulfills exact role quotas (e.g. 2 Audio, 1 Stage Lead)
• Filters out candidates beyond maximum distance

GOOGLE GEMINI API

Provides Human Explanations

• Synthesizes plain-English rationale for why candidates were chosen
• Highlights key trade-offs between cost and experience
• Isolated securely inside Supabase Edge Functions
